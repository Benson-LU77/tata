Tata 隱私政策 · Privacy Policy

中文

Tata 不收集任何資料。沒有帳號、沒有伺服器、沒有分析追蹤、沒有廣告。

你寫的每一個字都只存在你自己的裝置上:瀏覽器的本機書庫、你選的資料夾、你的 Obsidian 保管庫,或 iOS App 自己的文件夾。字不經過我們——我們根本沒有「經過」的地方。

App 不含第三方 SDK,不發出網路請求(除了你自己設定的本機 Obsidian 連線)。

English

Tata collects nothing. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads.

Every word you write stays on your own device: the browser's local bookcase, a folder you picked, your Obsidian vault, or the iOS app's own Documents folder. Your words never pass through us — there is no "us" for them to pass through.

The app contains no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests (other than the local Obsidian connection you configure yourself).
